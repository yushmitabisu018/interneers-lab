Return Policy

Eligibility:
Customers can return products within 7 days of delivery.

Conditions:
- Product must be unused and in original packaging.
- Receipt or proof of purchase is required.

Non-returnable Items:
- Damaged due to misuse
- Items without original packaging

Refund Process:
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after inspection.

Replacement:
- Replacement is available for defective products only.
