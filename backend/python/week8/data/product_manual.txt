Product Manual - Smart Toy Car

Overview:
The Smart Toy Car is a rechargeable remote-controlled car designed for kids aged 5 and above. It features obstacle detection, LED lights, and long battery life.

Features:
- Rechargeable battery (lasts up to 4 hours)
- Remote control range up to 10 meters
- Durable plastic body
- LED headlights

Usage Instructions:
1. Charge the toy car fully before first use.
2. Turn on the power switch located at the bottom.
3. Use the remote control to move the car forward, backward, left, and right.

Safety Instructions:
- Do not immerse in water.
- Avoid using on uneven surfaces.
- Keep away from fire.

Troubleshooting:
- If the car is not moving, check battery level.
- Ensure the remote batteries are working.
