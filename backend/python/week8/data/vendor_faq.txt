Vendor FAQ

Q1: How can I register as a vendor?
A: Vendors can register through the vendor portal by submitting required documents.

Q2: How are payments processed?
A: Payments are processed weekly to the registered bank account.

Q3: Can I update product details?
A: Yes, vendors can update product information from their dashboard.

Q4: What happens if a product is out of stock?
A: Vendors should update stock status regularly to avoid order issues.

Q5: How are returns handled?
A: Returned items are sent back to vendors after inspection.
